Capabilities

Built like a lab instrument.
Precise, quiet, fast.

Six pillars, engineered around one idea: the fastest possible path from question to mastery.

Native performance

A Rust core with near-instant startup and a tiny memory footprint. No Electron, no background services — just chemistry prep.

Mock exam simulator

Timed, section-based exams with negative marking, focus mode, real-time scoring and per-attempt results.

Complex chemistry rendering

Author in Tiptap, render LaTeX chemistry like \ce{H2O} as native MathML everywhere — in questions, solutions and notes.

Analytics engine

Accuracy, time distribution, weak-topic detection and performance trends — feeding a Mistake Room and an SM-2 spaced-repetition review.

Blazing global search

SQLite FTS5 across subjects, chapters, topics, tags and questions with fuzzy ranking — and a Ctrl+K command bar.

Duplicate & similarity detection

A signature-based similarity index with safe merge — keep your bank clean, keep every reference ID stable.

Showcase

A workspace that feels familiar from day one.

Every surface ships in the app's dark chrome — question bank, quiz, analytics and taxonomy.

Local-first & private

Your work stays on your machine.

Data lives in a user-owned workspace folder — portable USB included. No account to create, no cloud to sync, no telemetry reporting back, no data to sell.

  • No account Install, open a workspace, begin.
  • No cloud Fully offline. Your files, your folders.
  • No telemetry Nothing reports back. No analytics SDK.
  • Portable Carry your workspace on a USB stick.
